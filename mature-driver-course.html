What the course covers

A refresher built for experienced drivers

Driving rules, cars and roads keep changing. This course brings you up to date and helps you recognize how aging can affect your driving.

  • Practicing safe driving
  • The latest rules and regulations for safe driving
  • Recognizing how aging affects driving
  • Repairs and modifications to vehicles
  • Responding to changes in traffic and climate
  • The importance of keeping a good driving condition
  • Tips for driving with confidence
  • Transportation alternatives
