Minor's provisional license

License Requirements

To get a Minor's Provisional License in California, you must meet the following requirements.

16Minimum age
6 mo.Holding your instruction permit
50 hrsSupervised practice, incl. 10 at night
3Attempts at the driving test

  1. Age requirement

    Be at least 16 years old.

  2. Driver Education and Driver Training

    Complete both Driver Education and Driver Training. You'll receive two certificates:

    • Classroom Driver Education (DL 400B) or Online Driver Education (DL 400C). Take ours online
    • Driver Training (DL 400D). Our behind-the-wheel training

  3. Instruction permit

    Hold a California instruction permit for at least 6 months. See permit requirements

  4. Supervised driving practice

    Complete 50 hours of supervised driving practice, including 10 hours of night driving. A parent or guardian must confirm this.

  5. Behind-the-wheel driving test

    Pass the driving test. You have 3 attempts. If you fail, there's a 2-week waiting period, and a retest fee (currently $9) applies to the second and third attempts. Check current DMV fees

  6. Driving with a provisional license

    Once you have your provisional license, you can drive alone. For the first 12 months:

    • No driving between 11 PM and 5 AM.
    • No transporting passengers under 20 unless you're accompanied by a parent, guardian, or a licensed driver 25 or older.

  7. Exceptions to the restrictions

    Written exceptions may apply for medical, school, employment or family needs, signed by the right person (for example, a physician, school principal, employer or guardian).

    Emancipated minors don't need to provide this documentation, but must submit Proof of Financial Responsibility (SR 1P).
